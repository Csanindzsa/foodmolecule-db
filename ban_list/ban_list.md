# FoodMolecule-DB — Ban List

Foods listed here contain compounds that **cannot be effectively neutralized** through any common cooking or preparation method, or for which regulatory bans exist in multiple major jurisdictions.

> ⚠️ **Conditional bans** — Some entries are only dangerous in a specific form (unripe, raw, wrong species). These are marked as **CONDITIONAL**.

---

## Absolute Bans (Avoid Entirely)

### 1. Castor Beans
- **Harmful Molecule:** Ricin (abrin-class toxin)
- **Harm Level:** CRITICAL
- **Why:** 1–10 µg/kg body weight is lethal. No known antidote. Heat does NOT reliably inactivate ricin at domestic cooking temperatures.
- **Regulatory Status:** Controlled substance in most countries
- **Neutralizable:** ❌ No

### 2. Raw Bitter Almonds (Prunus amygdalus var. amara)
- **Harmful Molecule:** Amygdalin → Hydrogen Cyanide (HCN)
- **Harm Level:** CRITICAL
- **Why:** 50 raw bitter almonds can deliver a lethal dose of cyanide to an adult.
- **Note:** Sweet almonds (*P. amygdalus var. dulcis*) are safe.
- **Neutralizable:** ⚠️ Partial — roasting reduces HCN but may not eliminate to safe levels
- **Regulatory Status:** Sale restricted in EU and USA

### 3. Pennyroyal Oil (concentrated)
- **Harmful Molecule:** Pulegone
- **Harm Level:** CRITICAL
- **Why:** The essential oil (not the herb) causes liver failure. Often mistakenly used as an abortifacient.
- **Neutralizable:** ❌ No

## Conditional Bans (Safe ONLY Under Specific Conditions)

### 4. Puffer Fish / Fugu (unprepared)
- **Harmful Molecule:** Tetrodotoxin (TTX)
- **Harm Level:** CRITICAL
- **Why:** ~1.2 mg is lethal to a 70 kg adult. TTX is heat-stable and has no antidote.
- **Safe Condition:** Only when prepared by licensed fugu chefs in Japan with certified removal of toxic organs (liver, ovaries, skin).
- **Regulatory Status:** EU — banned. USA — import restricted. Japan — licensed preparation allowed.

### 5. Ackee (unripe or improperly prepared)
- **Harmful Molecule:** Hypoglycin A, Hypoglycin B
- **Harm Level:** CRITICAL
- **Why:** Causes Jamaican Vomiting Sickness — severe hypoglycemia, often fatal. The fruit is safe ONLY when fully ripe and the arils are opened naturally.
- **Safe Condition:** Ripe, naturally-opened arils only. Discard seeds and membranes.
- **Regulatory Status:** USA — fresh ackee banned from import. Canned ackee permitted.

### 6. Rhubarb Leaves
- **Harmful Molecule:** Oxalic acid (extremely high concentration), Anthraquinone glycosides
- **Harm Level:** HIGH-CRITICAL
- **Why:** Leaves contain 0.5–1% oxalic acid — many times higher than the stalks. Can cause kidney failure and death.
- **Safe Condition:** Stalks only — leaves must be discarded.
- **Neutralizable:** ❌ No (for the leaves; stalks are manageable)

### 7. Raw Kidney Beans (Phaseolus vulgaris)
- **Harmful Molecule:** Phytohaemagglutinin (PHA)
- **Harm Level:** HIGH
- **Why:** As few as 4–5 raw beans can cause severe nausea, vomiting, and diarrhea within 1–3 hours.
- **Safe Condition:** Must be boiled at 100°C for at least 10 minutes (slow cooking below boiling can *increase* toxicity).
- **Neutralizable:** ✅ Yes — with proper boiling

### 8. Star Fruit (Averrhoa carambola) — in kidney disease
- **Harmful Molecule:** Caramboxin (oxalic acid derivative)
- **Harm Level:** HIGH (for kidney-impaired individuals)
- **Why:** Causes neurotoxicity, seizures, and death in people with chronic kidney disease. Healthy kidneys can filter it.
- **Safe Condition:** Safe for people with healthy kidneys.

### 9. Ergot-contaminated Grain
- **Harmful Molecule:** Ergotamine, Ergometrine (ergot alkaloids)
- **Harm Level:** HIGH-CRITICAL
- **Why:** Causes ergotism — gangrenous or convulsive symptoms. Historically caused mass poisonings.
- **Safe Condition:** Modern grain inspection largely eliminates this risk; contaminated grain must be rejected.
- **Neutralizable:** ⚠️ Partial — some alkaloids are heat-labile but milling and visual inspection is the primary safeguard

## Substances to Minimize (Not Banned, But Limit Exposure)

| Food / Compound | Concern | Action |
|-----------------|---------|--------|
| Processed meats (nitrates/nitrites) | IARC Group 1 carcinogen (colorectal cancer) | Limit frequency; choose nitrate-free options |
| Refined vegetable oils (omega-6 heavy) | Promotes inflammation at excess | Balance with omega-3 sources |
| Acrylamide (fried/baked starchy foods) | IARC Group 2A probable carcinogen | Lower cooking temperature; avoid burning |
| Trans fats (partially hydrogenated oils) | Cardiovascular disease | Avoid entirely — banned in many countries |
| High-fructose corn syrup | Metabolic disease driver at high doses | Minimize processed food intake |
| Farmed salmon (PCBs, dioxins) | Bioaccumulated persistent organic pollutants | Limit to 1× per week; source matters |
