# Harm Type Tags

Every harmful molecule is tagged with one or more harm type identifiers. These tags allow filtering by mechanism of harm.

---

| Tag | Definition | Example Compounds |
|-----|------------|-------------------|
| `carcinogen` | Causes or promotes cancer. IARC Group 1 (known), 2A (probable), or 2B (possible). | Aflatoxin B1, Acrylamide, Benzopyrene |
| `endocrine_disruptor` | Mimics, blocks, or alters hormone signaling — especially estrogen, testosterone, thyroid. | BPA, Phytoestrogens (high dose), Atrazine |
| `neurotoxin` | Damages or disrupts the nervous system. | Tetrodotoxin, Lead, Mercury (methylmercury), Acrylamide |
| `hepatotoxin` | Damages liver cells or function. | Aflatoxin B1, Pyrrolizidine alkaloids, Pulegone, Ethanol (excess) |
| `nephrotoxin` | Damages kidneys or impairs renal function. | Oxalic acid (excess), Caramboxin, Aristolochic acid, Cadmium |
| `mineral_absorption_inhibitor` | Binds minerals (Fe, Zn, Ca, Mg) forming non-absorbable complexes. | Oxalic acid, Phytic acid, Tannins, EDTA |
| `gut_irritant` | Damages intestinal lining, disrupts gut barrier, or causes inflammation. | Gluten (in celiac disease), WGA lectin, Saponins, Capsaicin (excess) |
| `allergen` | Triggers IgE-mediated immune response in sensitized individuals. | Peanut allergens (Ara h1-17), Gluten, Shellfish tropomyosin |
| `inflammatory` | Promotes systemic chronic inflammation via COX pathway, NF-κB, or cytokine release. | Arachidonic acid (excess), Trans fats, Advanced glycation end products (AGEs) |
| `oxidative_stress` | Generates reactive oxygen species (ROS) or depletes antioxidant defenses. | Acrolein, 4-HNE (oxidized oils), Iron (pro-oxidant at excess) |
| `antinutrient` | Reduces the nutritional value of food by blocking absorption or digestion. | Phytic acid, Lectins, Trypsin inhibitors, Goitrogens |
| `drug_interaction` | Interferes with medication metabolism or efficacy. | Vitamin K1 (warfarin), Grapefruit furanocoumarins (CYP3A4) |
| `heavy_metal` | Toxic metal with bioaccumulation potential. | Lead, Mercury, Cadmium, Arsenic |
| `persistent_pollutant` | Environmental contaminant that accumulates in food chain. | PCBs, Dioxins, DDT |
| `nitrate_conversion_risk` | Can be converted to potentially harmful nitrosamines or methemoglobin-forming nitrite. | Nitrate (in infants), Nitrite (processed meats) |
| `kidney_stone_risk` | Promotes formation of urinary crystals (calcium oxalate, uric acid, etc.). | Oxalic acid, Purines → Uric acid |

---

*Tags are additive — a single molecule may have multiple tags.*
